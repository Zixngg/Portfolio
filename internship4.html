Dashboards in Power BI + Grafana

During my internship, I built multiple dashboards using Power BI and Grafana to help stakeholders monitor performance, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions faster. These dashboards replaced manual reporting workflows and improved visibility across different parts of the business.

In Power BI, I created structured reporting dashboards such as LSP and SSG tabulation views, as well as business-focused analytics like course profitability and MySkillsFuture market share tracking. I also built a geographic targeting dashboard to identify location-based patterns in sales and enquiries, supporting more targeted outreach and marketing decisions.

In Grafana, I created monitoring-style dashboards for continuous tracking, including company performance dashboards, SEO ranking tracking, and dashboards linked to internal systems such as the dynamic QR code generator. I also worked on analysing the relationship between sales and enquiries through dashboard visualisation to reveal conversion patterns and possible funnel drop-off points.

Across both tools, I prioritised usability and clarity: consistent layouts, meaningful filters, stakeholder-aligned metrics, and designs that support both quick checks and deeper analysis. I also supported ongoing maintenance as business needs and datasets evolved.

💡 Skills Demonstrated

  • Translating stakeholder questions into measurable KPIs
  • Building readable, reliable dashboards for real users
  • Data preparation and modelling for reporting
  • Continuous improvement and maintenance mindset

📊 Power BI Dashboards

1. Structured Reporting Dashboards

  • LSP Tabulation Views: Structured reporting for LSP-related data and metrics
  • SSG Tabulation Views: Comprehensive views for SSG-related reporting and analysis

2. Business Analytics Dashboards

  • Course Profitability: Analytics dashboard tracking course profitability metrics and trends
  • MySkillsFuture Market Share: Tracking and analysis of market share performance on MySkillsFuture platform
  • Geographic Targeting: Dashboard identifying location-based patterns in sales and enquiries to support targeted outreach and marketing decisions

📈 Grafana Dashboards

1. Performance Monitoring

  • Company Performance Dashboards: Continuous tracking of company performance metrics and KPIs
  • SEO Ranking Tracking: Monitoring and visualisation of SEO ranking trends and indicators

2. System Integration Dashboards

  • Dynamic QR Code Generator Monitoring: Dashboards linked to the internal dynamic QR code system to track engagement and performance
  • Sales & Enquiries Analysis: Visualisation of the relationship between sales and enquiries to reveal conversion patterns and identify funnel drop-off points

🎨 Design Principles

I prioritised usability and clarity across all dashboards:

  • Consistent Layouts: Standardised design patterns for easy navigation and familiarity
  • Meaningful Filters: Filters aligned with stakeholder needs and common analysis scenarios
  • Stakeholder-Aligned Metrics: KPIs and metrics that directly answer business questions
  • Dual-Purpose Design: Dashboards supporting both quick checks and deeper analysis
  • Maintainability: Ongoing support and updates as business needs and datasets evolve

💼 Business Impact

These dashboards replaced manual reporting workflows, significantly reducing the time required for data analysis and decision-making. The improved visibility across different parts of the business enabled stakeholders to identify trends, monitor performance, and make data-driven decisions faster. The geographic targeting dashboard, in particular, supported more effective marketing and outreach strategies.
