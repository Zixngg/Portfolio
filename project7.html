AI Transcription & Translation Support for Videotto

I assisted a friend in developing AI-powered transcription and translation features for Videotto, an AI-based video editing service. The platform focuses on automating video post-production workflows, where speech-to-text transcription and multilingual translation play a key role in enabling subtitles, content repurposing, and accessibility.

My contributions focused on Chinese-language transcription, English-to-Chinese translation support, and integration guidance, helping ensure the AI outputs were accurate, usable, and suitable for real-world video editing scenarios.

🔧 My Contributions

Chinese Speech-to-Text Support

I helped research and evaluate AI transcription approaches for Mandarin Chinese, taking into account:

  • Tonal language accuracy
  • Sentence segmentation and punctuation
  • Handling continuous speech and varied speaking speeds
  • Suitability for subtitle and caption generation

This ensured that the transcription output could be reliably used for editing and subtitle workflows.

English to Chinese Translation Integration

In addition to transcription, I assisted with English-to-Chinese translation workflows, enabling:

  • Automatic translation of English transcripts into Chinese subtitles
  • Multilingual content support for a wider audience
  • Faster turnaround for creators producing bilingual or Chinese-language content

I helped assess translation quality and identify common issues such as mistranslations, unnatural phrasing, and context loss, ensuring outputs were more natural and audience-appropriate.

Integration & Workflow Design Support

I supported the overall AI integration by:

  • Advising on how transcription and translation outputs could be structured for frontend use
  • Helping design workflows where text outputs could be reused for subtitles, captions, and content editing
  • Identifying edge cases such as timing alignment, filler words, and translation consistency

This helped ensure the AI features fit smoothly into Videotto's broader editing pipeline.

Testing & Iteration

I contributed by:

  • Testing transcription and translation accuracy across different video types
  • Providing feedback on errors related to pronunciation, pacing, and translation quality
  • Assisting with iterative improvements to improve reliability and usability
